Ward-Specific Clinical Protocols

The platform moves beyond basic alert signals, presenting custom, structured operational checklists relative to specific ward environments.

Medical Ward

Vital Evaluation Protocols

"VITAL SIGNS CALL - Check and record BP, HR, O2, Temperature, Respiratory Rate."

Maintains rigid, cyclical execution frameworks to capture variations across unstable multi-pathology cases.

ICU (Intensive Care)

Critical Life Support Protocols

"CRITICAL CARE CALL - Check ventilator parameters. Verify endotracheal tube placement security."

Direct integration with life support systems minimizes cognitive strain and optimizes response under extreme duress.

NICU (Neonatal ICU)

Thermoregulation & Feeding

"NEONATAL CARE - Confirm incubator internal environment matches (36.5-37.5°C). Administer precise fluid intake schedule."

Protects fragile neonatal patients by asserting tight environmental and nourishment alert barriers.

Pediatric Ward

Volumetric Dosage Controls

"MEDICATION CALL - Give Paracetamol 120mg Oral. Weight: 8kg. Calculated: 15mg/kg x 8kg = 120mg. Verified safe."

Automates secondary verification of pediatric medicine tracking variables to remove calculation deviations.

Isolation Ward

Pathogen Safety Enforcement

"MEDICATION CALL - Full personal protection required: N95 respirator, gown, gloves, shield. Enter anteroom zone first."

Enforces dynamic containment checklists prior to contact to protect operating healthcare personnel.
